How to read this map

Each colour is a controlling authority (the SUBJECTO field), so an empire's provinces share its colour. Solid borders are precise, dashed ones moderately precise, and faint dotted ones approximate (the dataset's BORDERPRECISION 3, 2 and 1).

Borders before the modern era are estimates, often disputed, and often drawn where no one at the time drew a line. Read the map as a picture of what historians think, not a record of sovereignty.
