A historical atlas

Every border is written over the last.

A palimpsest is a page scraped clean and written on again, the old lines still showing through. These are the world's borders from 123,000 BC to 2010 AD, 54 snapshots in all, nine of them stacked here.

Open the atlas Browse every era

    Three ways to look

    Atlas, map, globe.

    A flat Natural Earth projection for the whole world at once, a slippy map to zoom into a coastline, and a globe to turn by hand. Every view shares the same year.

    Honest borders

    Faint where it's guessed.

    The dataset rates its borders, and mostly per era: older snapshots are marked approximate and drawn faint and dotted, recent ones precise and drawn solid. Colours follow who controlled a region within a year.

    Open data

    Built on a living dataset.

    The maps come from André Ourednik's historical-basemaps, corrected by many contributors. New snapshots arrive here by a clean merge.
